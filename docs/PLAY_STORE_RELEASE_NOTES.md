# Play Store release notes

Use this text in the Play Console **What's new** field for version 2.0.0 (code 24). It is kept under 500 characters.

## 2.0.0

Set up Immich TV from your phone. Scan the QR on the TV, enter your Immich server and sign-in details on the phone, and finish sign-in on the TV.

- No pairing code or approval step — scanning connects right away.
- Use your phone as a remote: D-pad, Select/Back, text entry, and playback controls. Multiple phones can control the TV at once.
- Cleaner TV navigation, resizable dialogs, and an animated app logo.

Use phone setup and remote control only on a trusted home network.
